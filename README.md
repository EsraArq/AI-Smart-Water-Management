# Smart Water Management: AI, IoT, and Cybersecurity

A graduate research literature review exploring how artificial intelligence and Internet of Things technologies can support water management, alongside the cybersecurity risks of connected water infrastructure.

**Author:** Esra Abuarqob  
**Institution:** Coastal Carolina University  
**Course:** IST650D1 — Information Systems Technology in Context  
**Paper date:** July 3, 2024

## Research paper

**The Future of Water: Advancements and Cybersecurity Challenges in Smart Water Management System (SWMS) Technology**

The paper examines potential applications of connected sensors and analytical techniques in water conservation, water-quality monitoring, leak detection, demand forecasting, and distribution management. It also discusses cybersecurity threats and layered security measures for smart water infrastructure.

## Topics covered

- Sensor monitoring of water flow, pressure, quality, and consumption.
- Discussion of Isolation Forest, k-nearest neighbors, LSTM networks, ARIMA, and optimization techniques in water-management applications.
- Published examples involving South Bend wastewater management, Aquasuite, and agricultural irrigation.
- Threats including unauthorized access, ransomware, phishing, and system tampering.
- Security measures including network segmentation, firewalls, access controls, encryption, monitoring, and patch management.

## Scope

This is a literature-based graduate research project. It discusses technologies, published case studies, threats, and security approaches; it does not present a working application, trained model, original dataset, or experimentally validated system. No numerical performance or water-savings results are claimed here as the author's own findings.

This repository currently provides a research summary. The full paper is not included in this release.
